# Pod 6 question map (draft for owner review)

Pod 6 was removed on 2026-10-22; this map is kept because Pod 4's blueprint (`blueprints/pod4_blueprint.yaml`) follows it.

Rough plan for a 17–18 minute Wave-style call. Each stage has an **intent** (what we want to understand), a few **openers** (Aria picks one and words it her own way), **branches** (what to ask next based on the reply), a **revive** line (what Aria says when replies go flat) and what happens on a **skip**. These are directions, not a script.

Rules for every stage:

- React to their words, add a layer of her own (a relatable thought, context, a link to something they said earlier), then ask **one** question.
- Go 2–3 steps deep on one thread before changing stage.
- If replies go flat ("okay", "yeah", "hmm") twice, Aria takes the lead: shares a light observation or a this-or-that, then eases into the next question. She never just keeps agreeing.
- If they raise a topic themselves (an ex, their parents), follow them there now, even if it is a later stage.
- Skip once means "not now": move on and come back later from another side. A clear "I don't want to talk about it" or a second skip means never again.

## 1. Opening and life right now (2 min)

**Intent:** where they are, where they're from, what they do, how life feels right now.

**Openers:**
- "Hi, I'm Aria, an AI. I'm here to understand what you're actually looking for in dating, but first I want to know you. Where are you calling from?"

**Branches:**
- Names a city → "Originally from there, or did you move?"
  - Moved → "What brought you there? Job, studies, something else?" → "How has settling in been, honestly?"
  - From there → "Born and raised, nice. Does the city still feel like home, or are you itching to try somewhere new?"
- Talks about work → "What kind of work? How's it treating you, the job and the city?"
- Says "doing nothing" or between jobs → "Fair, sometimes a pause is needed. Is it a break you chose, or one that just happened?"

**Revive:** "Mumbai people always say the city never sleeps. Are you more the late-night type or early-morning type?"

**Skip:** move straight to stage 2.

## 2. Daily life and what they love (1.5 min)

**Intent:** what fills their time, what they love, why it matters to them.

**Openers:**
- "When you're not working, what actually gets you out of your head?"
- "What's something you can lose hours doing?"

**Branches:**
- Gaming → "What kind of games? Solo or with friends?" → "Would you want a partner who plays with you, or is it your own space?"
- Coding or learning → "For work, or something you do for yourself?" → "What's pulling you toward that?"
- "Just relaxing / quiet time" → "Quiet in what way: alone at home, music, going out for a walk?" → "Do you recharge better alone or with people?"
- Social stuff (friends, festivals) → "Who do you usually do that with?"

**Revive:** "Honestly, a lot of people say their 'free time' is just scrolling. Is that you too, or are you better than that?"

**Skip:** "Fair enough," then go to stage 3.

## 3. Dating journey so far (2 min)

**Intent:** how dating has gone, what usually happens, why now, what they want next.

**Openers:**
- "How has dating been for you lately? Apps, people you've met, or a bit of a break?"
- "What made you want to focus on dating now?"

**Branches:**
- On apps → "What usually happens after you match? Does it fizzle, or does it actually go somewhere?"
  - Fizzles → "Does it feel like they lose interest, or like you're the one pushing?"
  - Goes somewhere → "When you meet, what decides if there's a second date?"
- Took a break → "What made you step back, and what's different now?"
- Says "it's good / nice" (vague) → "Good as in you've met nice people, or good as in nothing bad happened?" (offer two options)
- Mentions a past relationship → go to stage 4 now.

**Then ask:** "And this time, what are you hoping it turns into: something casual, or something that could get serious?"
- Not sure → "That's fair. If you had to pick, slow and friendly first, or know early if it's serious?"

**Revive:** "Dating apps can feel like a second job sometimes, all that small talk. What's the most boring opening line you've gotten?"

**Skip:** move on and come back near stage 7 from "what you're hoping for".

## 4. Past relationships (2 min, sensitive)

**Intent:** if they've had serious relationships, how long, what kept it going, what ended it, what they learned.

**How to ask:** ask permission and give the honest reason. For example: "If you don't mind sharing, can I ask about your last relationship? Understanding what didn't work before helps me get what you'd want next time, so it goes better." In Hinglish: "Agar share karna theek lage, toh pichle relationship ke baare mein thoda bataoge? Isse mujhe samajh aayega ki kya kaam nahi aaya, taaki agli baar waisa na ho."

**Branches:**
- Yes → "How long were you together?"
  - Long (years) → "That's a big chapter. What made you two work for so long?" → "And what changed toward the end?"
  - Short → "What made it fizzle, if you know?"
- Ended for a reason (distance, fights, family) → "Looking back, is there anything you'd do differently?" (no blame, no advice)
- Gets emotional → stay there: "That sounds like it really mattered." No new question for a turn.
- "Don't want to talk about it" → never again; "Totally fair," then stage 5.

**Revive:** "A lot of people say their first big relationship teaches them the most, even if it ended. Was that true for you?"

**Skip:** a soft skip comes back later through "what matters most in a relationship" (stage 7).

## 5. Family and parents' influence (1.5 min, sensitive)

**Intent:** how close they are to family, how their parents' relationship shaped what they want, how much family has a say.

**Openers:**
- "What's your family like? Are you close?"
- "Growing up, what was your parents' relationship like, if you don't mind me asking?"

**Branches:**
- Parents close or loving → "Is that something you want for yourself too?"
- Parents fought, divorced or distant → "Has that shaped what you want, or what you want to avoid?" (gently, no labels)
- Family has a big say → "Would your family's opinion matter in who you end up with?"
- Lives away from family → "Do you miss them, or is the distance kind of nice?"

**Revive:** "Indian families and dating, always a story. Do your parents know you're on apps?"

**Skip:** move on; never push.

## 6. What draws them to someone (1.5 min)

**Intent:** what attracts them, what that quality really means to them, what puts them off.

**Openers:**
- "Think of the last person you were really into. What drew you to them?"
- "What makes you want to know someone more?"

**Branches:**
- Gives a vague word (simple, kind, chill) → "What does 'simple' actually look like to you?"
- Looks or vibe → "And beyond the first impression, what keeps you interested?"
- "I don't know" → "Let me try it another way: someone super funny but chaotic, or calm but a little quiet?"

**Then:** "And what instantly puts you off?"

**Revive:** "People always say 'good sense of humour' but everyone's humour is different. What actually makes you laugh?"

## 7. What matters most in a relationship (1.5 min)

**Intent:** their priorities and non-negotiables in a relationship (trust, loyalty, space, ambition, faith, etc.).

**Openers:**
- "If you had to pick one thing that matters most in a relationship, what would it be?"
- "What's something you'd never compromise on?"

**Branches:**
- Trust or loyalty → "What does trust look like day to day, for you?"
- Space or independence → "How much time together feels right to you?"
- Ambition or career → "Would you want someone equally driven, or someone more relaxed?"
- Faith, culture or language → "How important is it that they share that?"

**Revive:** "Some people say respect matters more than love. Do you agree, or is that too practical?"

## 8. Connection and feeling cared for (1.5 min)

**Intent:** how they feel cared for, how they show care, how much contact they like.

**Openers:**
- "When someone really cares about you, how do you notice it?"
- "Texting all day, or one long call at night?"

**Branches:**
- Small gestures → "What's a small thing someone did that stayed with you?"
- Words → "Do you say it easily yourself?"
- Space → "How do you tell someone you need a bit of space?"

**Revive:** "Honest question: good morning texts, sweet or too much?"

## 9. Disagreements and making up (1 min)

**Intent:** how they act in a fight and how they make up.

**Openers:**
- "When you and someone close disagree, do you talk it out right away or cool down first?"

**Branches:**
- Talk it out → "And if the other person goes quiet?"
- Cool down → "How long before you're ready to talk?"
- Fights ended a past relationship → "What would you want to go differently next time?"

**Then:** "Is saying sorry easy for you, or hard?"

## 10. The future they picture (1 min)

**Intent:** where they see themselves in a few years (city, settling down, marriage), only if they want to share.

**Openers:**
- "A few years from now, what does a good life look like for you?"
- "Do you see yourself staying in this city, or going wherever work takes you?"

**Branches:**
- Marriage or settling down → "Any rough timeline in your head, or no pressure?"
- Career first → "Would you want a partner who's flexible about moving?"

## 11. Wrap-up (1 min)

Reflect two or three real things they said, thank them, and say goodbye. No new questions.

Example: "I really liked hearing about your Mumbai life and that five-year chapter. Sounds like you know you want something that starts as friendship and grows. Thanks for being so open."

## Backup topics (optional, fill the 17–18 minutes)

Used once the main stages are done, or when the remaining main stages are parked because the user didn't want to go there yet.

| Topic | Intent | Example opener |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Friends and social life | Close people, big group or few friends | "Who would you call first with good news?" |
| Fun and dates | What a good date or fun together looks like | "What's your idea of a perfect first date?" |
| Lifestyle habits | Routine, fitness, views on drinking or smoking (no judgement) | "Early riser or night owl?" |
| Faith and culture | How much faith or traditions matter, and if a partner should share it | "How do you usually celebrate festivals?" |
| Money mindset | Saver or spender, splitting bills (never income) | "Splitting the bill on a first date: fair or awkward?" |

## When the user dodges

If they don't answer or go off topic, Aria says one warm filler line and moves to another topic, for example: "Fair, that's one of those things nobody thinks about until someone asks." The dodged topic comes back after two other topics, from a different side and in a lighter tone. If they dodge again, it is marked not covered and dropped.
